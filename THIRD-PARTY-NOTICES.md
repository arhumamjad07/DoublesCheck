# Third-party components and data

Meterless H-MEM reference implementation is Apache-2.0, pinned to commit
1742dec56a66e58eef9dda2d2b865823758ca8cd. Its full license and source manifest
are retained in vendor/hmem/LICENSE and vendor/hmem/UPSTREAM.json.

Pokemon Showdown 0.11.11 and its historical Regulation H format definition are
MIT licensed. Copyright (c) 2011-2026 Guangcong Luo and other contributors.
The license is included in licenses/pokemon-showdown-MIT.txt and the package.
The historical rules definition is attributed by URL and commit in data/sources.json.

Smogon aggregate ladder statistics are sourced from the public November 2024
statistics archive, attributed in data/sources.json. They describe the named
historical ladder and are not official Pokemon championship results. We do not
claim ownership of third-party data or grant rights beyond their owners' terms.

Pokemon names and other franchise marks belong to their respective owners.
This is an unofficial fan tool; no official artwork is included.

Transitive npm dependencies retain their own license files in node_modules
when installed. package-lock.json pins the dependency graph and integrity hashes.
